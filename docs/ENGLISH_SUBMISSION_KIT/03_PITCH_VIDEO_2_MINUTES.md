# Founder pitch video — English script (target 1:45–1:55)

**Recording rule:** Make this a separate founder-facing pitch video, no more than two minutes to fit the stricter live-form guidance. The official general FAQ describes a two-to-three-minute video; a sub-two-minute version satisfies both. Actual speaking speed varies: rehearse with a timer.

### 00:00–00:20 — Opening / problem

"Every important team decision contains a prediction. Will a launch succeed? Will demand rise? Will a new strategy work? But six months later, teams often cannot explain what they believed, why they believed it, or whether they were right."

### 00:20–00:45 — What we built

"I'm building OutcomeOS, a decision-intelligence workspace that turns uncertain questions into a record teams can learn from. Teams create measurable YES-or-NO questions, record forecasts, collect evidence, and compare their expectations over time."

### 00:45–01:10 — Why Panta

"Panta provides the real prediction-market layer. OutcomeOS discovers Panta markets, retrieves quoted prices, and connects relevant contracts to a team's decision. Instead of using markets only for trading, we use market signals to help organizations evaluate the quality of their thinking."

### 01:10–01:35 — Product and differentiation

"We have built collaborative workspaces, market-quote history, divergence alerts, outcome scoring, and privacy-preserving shared reports. Our integration is read-only: no wallet, custody, or trading is required. The value is not a new market screen, but a complete feedback loop from forecast to result."

### 01:35–01:55 — Who / next steps

"We are starting with founders, research teams, and product operators. Next, we will validate demand through pilots and weekly forecast-review sessions. OutcomeOS helps teams remember their reasoning, learn from real outcomes, and make more informed decisions. Thank you."

## Recording notes

- Introduce yourself with your **real name and actual background** in the opening or closing. If needed, reduce one sentence above to stay under two minutes.
- Say "functional beta" instead of "production SaaS" until public deployment and live-key tests are complete.
- Do **not** say "AI-powered prediction engine": this release uses market data and ordinary scoring, not a trained prediction model.
- Record separately from the screen-capture demonstration. Keep the speaker visible and audio clean.
- Do not name customers, pilot teams, or investors who have not agreed to be named.
