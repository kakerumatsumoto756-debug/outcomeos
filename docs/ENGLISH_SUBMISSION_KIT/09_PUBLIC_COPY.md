## 40-second product explanation
OutcomeOS helps teams create auditable forecasts about uncertain events. You set a measurable question, deadline, and resolution rules; teammates add probability estimates and evidence; and a relevant Panta market can provide external quote context. OutcomeOS records the timeline and scores the forecasts after resolution. It turns market information into a learning workflow, not a betting workflow.
