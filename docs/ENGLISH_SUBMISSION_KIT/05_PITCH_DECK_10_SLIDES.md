# OutcomeOS — Ten-slide investor/judge pitch deck copy (English)

## Slide 1 — OutcomeOS
**Turn predictions into better decisions.**
A decision-learning workspace powered by Panta prediction-market intelligence.
Visual: product logo + authentic application screenshot.

## Slide 2 — The problem
**Teams make predictions. They rarely learn from them.**
Business decisions live in chat threads, meeting notes, and disconnected dashboards. Probability estimates are forgotten; evidence changes; hindsight makes outcomes seem obvious.
Visual: three disconnected notes -> incomplete decision loop.

## Slide 3 — Why now
**Market intelligence is becoming programmable.**
Prediction markets provide structured prices and settlement rules. Panta enables programmatic market discovery and quote retrieval. The opportunity is to take the data beyond a trading screen and into team workflows.
Visual: Panta API -> organizational decisions.

## Slide 4 — The solution
**One timeline from forecast to outcome.**
Create measurable YES/NO decisions, collect forecasts, save evidence, connect compatible Panta markets, monitor quotes, and score the result.
Visual: six-step workflow.

## Slide 5 — Live product
**Market data meets team decisions.**
Show one screenshot of a real Panta market detail in OutcomeOS and one screenshot of a decision room. Use authentic live results; label any synthetic onboarding data.
Visual: two screenshots with annotations.

## Slide 6 — Why we are different
**Not a trading terminal. A decision memory layer.**
Traders focus on entering positions. OutcomeOS focuses on whether a team's forecast was reasoned, tracked, and learned from. No wallet, custody, or trade execution is needed.
Visual: trading interface versus decision workflow (no unverified competitor claims).

## Slide 7 — Our technology
**Panta provides market signals; OutcomeOS closes the loop.**
Python API service; server-side Panta authentication; market discovery and quote snapshots; team forecasts and scoring; JavaScript UI; PostgreSQL cloud storage.
Visual: architecture from UI -> OutcomeOS backend -> Panta API + database.

## Slide 8 — Initial customers and validation
**Start where probabilistic decisions are frequent.**
Founders, product teams, and research groups. First milestone: run pilot interviews and weekly decision reviews. Track repeat usage, recurring active teams, and whether reports change decisions.
Visual: pilot workflow; do not invent user counts or letters of intent.

## Slide 9 — Business model
**A team subscription, if pilots validate value.**
Free individual usage -> team subscriptions for persistent workspaces and advanced reports -> enterprise access and audit controls. This is a proposed model, not existing revenue.
Visual: product-led adoption journey.
