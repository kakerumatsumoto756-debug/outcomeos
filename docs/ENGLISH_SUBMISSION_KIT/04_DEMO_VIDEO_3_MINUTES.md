## Choose an honest market example

- The same YES/NO event and settlement deadline must be used in both your OutcomeOS decision and linked Panta market. Never imply a different question is the same market.
- A candidate in an earlier local screenshot was an Arsenal vs Leeds United football market dated October 10, 2026, but recheck its actual availability, phase, and settlement rules before recording. This is an illustrative candidate, **not a promise of a live market**.
- If there is no suitable market, demonstrate discovery and quote details, acknowledge the mismatch, and leave the decision unlinked. Never fabricate a market or quote.
- For a scoring example, use an already resolved **historical outcome with evidence**, or label synthetic scoring clearly as a test scenario.

## Video production safety

- Hide Panta keys, database URLs, passwords, private company data, and browser password popups.
- Make both videos accessible to judges without requesting permissions.
- Presentation video and product demo video are separate uploads.
- Prefer clear 720p/1080p screen recording, stable cursor, and subtitles if speaking quickly.
